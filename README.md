Oct 29, 2026, Data Storytelling with Adobe Express
Work sample for Renee Castillo, Director of Card Operations at Bear Down Bank: replace 440 non-contactless cards early for $3,520.
[Work sample (PDF)](https://github.com/manasi-varaiya/mis561-portfolio/blob/main/adobe/varaiya-data-storytelling.pdf) · [Adobe Express](https://new.express.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:US:dc808f38-d1e6-415b-bce7-b095b38dc515?accept=true&invite=true&promoid=Z2G1FQKR&mv=other)
I used a month-by-month bar chart with the 440 cards in orange and the 1,253 cards expiring within six months in gray, because Renee's decision is where to draw the line on early replacement, and the chart shows the batch as a thin tail of 32 to 55 cards a month through month 15 next to the near-term wave. I considered a card-type table alone, but it could not show timing, so I kept it as a small supporting element. I removed the "440 cards × $8 = $3,520" annotation box from the chart; the headline already states the cost, and removing it left one clean visual with nothing competing for the eye.
